FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Visit Support or email support@mamababybites.com.

Do I have to follow the app every day to introduce foods?

No. The app is here to guide you, not to keep score. Go at your baby's pace, skip a day or repeat a week, and choose foods that fit your schedule and your family's tastes.

Can I use the app if we have food allergies or a special diet?

Yes. When you set up the app, tell it about allergies in your household and diets like vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher or gluten-free. Every recipe shows its allergens and which diets it suits, and you can hide recipes with the allergens you avoid. If your baby has eczema or a known food allergy, talk to your pediatrician before introducing common allergens.

What age is best for using the app?

From newborn days to age 3. The monthly guides start at birth, the starting-solids plan begins when your baby is ready for solids (usually around 6 months), and the recipes grow with your child, with family meals for everyone.

Does it work for breastfeeding and formula feeding?

Yes. You tell the app how you're feeding, and the guides and plans work for breast milk, formula or both.

Can my partner or other caregivers use it too?

Yes, they can install Mama & Baby Bites on their own iPhone. There's no account, so each phone keeps its own plans and food log, and nothing is synced between devices. You can send your shopping list to anyone from the app with a tap.

Do I need an account? Where is my data stored?

No account needed. Your baby's details, food log, plans and lists are saved only on your iPhone. When you use Ask, your question and your baby's age in months are sent to write an answer, and the chat isn't saved. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

How does the free trial work?

Start a 7-day free trial of Premium in the app. You won't be charged during the trial, and you can cancel any time in your iPhone Settings at least 24 hours before it ends. The free version stays available whether or not you subscribe.

Is it available on Android?

Mama & Baby Bites is launching on iPhone first. Email us at support@mamababybites.com if you'd like to hear about other platforms.
